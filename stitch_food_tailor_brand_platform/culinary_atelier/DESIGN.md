## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intimacy, discretion, and exacting standards of a bespoke private culinary concierge. It draws inspiration from fine bookbinding, high-end gastronomy monographs, and architectural gallery spaces. The aesthetic moves decisively away from transactional food delivery marketplaces—shedding frantic countdown timers, aggressive badge clusters, and saturated primary buttons—in favor of an editorial, print-grade digital atelier.

Key aesthetic pillars:
- **Paper & Ink Discipline**: Interfaces are constructed as layered sheets of heavyweight, uncoated warm paper (`#FAF9F6`, `#F7F3EC`) inscribed with deep evergreen botanical ink (`#173E23`), punctuated sparingly with vermilion-tinted tailored orange (`#C55418`) editorial markers.
- **Typographic Restraint**: Generous vertical white space, classical proportioning, and rigorous hierarchy mimic luxury print layouts. High-contrast typography pairs quiet, expansive headline scales with tracked micro-labels.
- **Architectural Tactility**: Structure is defined by hairline rules (1px `#E5E0D8`) and planar transitions rather than artificial elevations or drop shadows.
- **Intentionality Over Density**: Generous negative space gives culinary photography and curated tasting menus space to breathe. Every UI element exists strictly to serve the patron's focus.

## Colors

The palette operates on strict ratio allocation to maintain an elevated, editorial tone: **70–80% neutral paper foundations**, **15–25% deep forest ink**, and **5–10% accent orange** reserved strictly for directional focus and editorial stamps.

### Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF9F6`)**: The primary viewport canvas, evoking tactile, unbleached cotton paper.
- **Elevated Paper (`#FFFFFF`)**: Used for foreground cards, interactive focus modules, and high-clarity content wells.
- **Warm Atelier (`#F7F3EC`)**: A rich, tonal neutral used for structured sidebars, table summaries, inset meta-panels, and active hover states.
- **Hairline Border (`#E5E0D8`)**: A warm, low-contrast structural border that delineates spatial zones without harsh visual friction.

### Ink & Content Hierarchy
- **Primary Ink (`#173E23`)**: Deep forest green serving as the primary text, high-emphasis display titles, and prominent container fills. It replaces traditional black to give the interface an organic, bespoke depth.
- **Muted Editorial (`#4B6353`)**: Secondary text color for deck copy, ingredient details, and contextual captions.
- **Tertiary Stencil (`#5C7262`)**: Subtle metadata, table headers, inactive iconography, and disabled states.
- **Inverse Crisp (`#FFFFFF`)**: High-contrast legibility against deep green containers and dark interactive fills.

### Accents & Signifiers
- **Tailored Orange (`#C55418`)**: An energetic yet grounded vermilion accent used for active curation badges, primary interactive anchors, selection indicators, and sommelier callouts. Never used over large background areas.

## Typography

The type system relies exclusively on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, deployed with careful typographic contrast to evoke high-end editorial magazines rather than standard software interfaces.

- **Headlines & Titles**: Rendered in sentence case with tight, intentional tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.025em`) and balanced line heights. This provides a warm, literary character without stiff formal archaism.
- **Body & Prose**: Set with generous line spacing (minimum 1.6× line-height ratio) to ensure long-form menu compositions, dietary notes, and chef profiles remain effortless to read.
- **Editorial Micro-Labels**: Used for category tags, provenance notices, step indicators, and cellar vintages. These always appear in uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.14em`) and medium-to-semibold weights, replicating traditional book folio markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure takes cues from classical editorial composition: asymmetric balances, deliberate margins, and generous breathing room around visual assets.

### Grid Rhythm & Margins
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column layout with 32px (`2rem`) gutters and 56px (`3.5rem`) outer page margins. Maximum readable container width caps at 1340px to preserve editorial balance.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) margins.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px)**: 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) edge margins.

### Spatial Discipline
- **Section Spacing**: Major editorial chapters transition using `space-3xl` (72px) to `space-4xl` (96px) on desktop, scaling to `space-2xl` (48px) on mobile.
- **Content Stacking**: Grouped meta-information relies on `space-xs` (8px) and `space-sm` (12px), while distinct narrative blocks separate cleanly at `space-xl` (32px).
- **Asymmetric Offsets**: Chef biographies and featured course displays utilize offset column spans (e.g., 5-column image / 7-column narrative) to establish a natural editorial cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system categorically rejects heavy blur drop shadows, colorful underglows, and 3D neomorphic extrusions. Depth is communicated purely through **tonal stratification** and **crisp hairline separation**.

### Depth Layers
1. **Foundation Level (0)**: Viewport background rendered in `#FAF9F6`.
2. **Structural Layer (1)**: Surface modules, menu groupings, and dialogue cards rendered in `#FFFFFF`, bound by a crisp 1px solid `#E5E0D8` border.
3. **Recessed Wells (-1)**: Inset tasting notes, chef commentary sidebars, and input containers washed in `#F7F3EC`.
4. **Transient Floating Layer (2)**: Dropdowns, date pickers, and bespoke modal sheets feature a 1px `#E5E0D8` border and a delicate, warm-tinted ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(23, 62, 35, 0.05), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(23, 62, 35, 0.03)`.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects architectural book arts: crisp, grounded, and restrained. Pill shapes (`rounded-full`) are strictly forbidden across interactive controls to prevent a generic consumer app aesthetic.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Tags, Menu Items)**: Defined at `6px` to `8px` (`0.375rem` to `0.5rem`).
- **Cards, Panels & Media Frames**: Set to `10px` to `12px` (`0.625rem` to `0.75rem`), introducing just enough softness to complement high-resolution culinary photography.
- **Hero Framing & Modals**: Maximize corner curve at `12px`. Never exceed `12px` anywhere in the design.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#173E23` background with sharp `#FFFFFF` typography (`label-md`). Radius set to 8px. Padding is balanced and expansive: 14px vertical, 24px horizontal. Hover transitions smoothly to an active tint (`rgba(23, 62, 35, 0.92)`).
- **Editorial Accent Action**: Solid `#C55418` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, applied exclusively to high-conviction decision paths such as final booking confirmations or sommelier reservations.
- **Secondary (Paper Outline)**: Surface `#FFFFFF` or transparent, bounded by a 1px `#E5E0D8` rule. Text is `#173E23`. Hover shifts the background to `#F7F3EC`.
- **Tertiary / Link**: No border or background fill. Inscribed in `#173E23` with subtle underline or trailing arrow indicator, transitioning to `#C55418` on interaction.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- **Status & Attribute Chips**: Rectangular with 6px radius. Neutral base: `#F7F3EC` fill, `#4B6353` text, 1px `#E5E0D8` stroke.
- **Curator Tags**: Reserved for signature dishes and vintage selections. `#FAF9F6` fill with an explicit `#C55418` text color and subtle 1px `#C55418` tinted border. Typography strictly conforms to `label-editorial`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Text Inputs**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#E5E0D8` border, 8px radius. Text in `#173E23`. Placeholder rendered in `#5C7262`. Focused state transitions border to `#173E23` without glow rings.
- **Form Labels**: Always positioned above the field, formatted in `label-editorial` uppercase using `#4B6353`.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px square (or circle for radios) with 4px radius. Default state: `#FFFFFF` fill with 1.5px `#E5E0D8` border. Checked state: `#173E23` fill with sharp white checkmark or center pin.

### Cards & Tasting Menu Containers
- **Culinary Cards**: Composed with `#FFFFFF` background and a continuous 1px `#E5E0D8` stroke. Inner padding ranges from 24px to 32px. Imagery retains a 6px corner radius and is accompanied by editorial category labeling placed directly above the title.
- **Course Timeline Cards**: Borderless or left-bordered with a refined 2px `#173E23` anchor bar, placing ingredient lists in `body-sm` `#4B6353` directly below course titles.
